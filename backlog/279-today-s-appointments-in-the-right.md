# 279 Today's appointments in the right sidebar

As a desktop user, I want the day's appointments at the very top of the right sidebar, each opening a page of its related notes, so that I can get to the notes for my next meeting from anywhere.

## What

At the very top of the right sidebar, above the outline: a heading showing Today (the date on other days) with previous-day and next-day arrows next to it. On today, list the last past appointment plus every later appointment that day; on any other day, list all appointments. Each appointment is clickable and opens an appointment page that links all notes related to it. Appointments with identical names are the same appointment and open the same page. If the appointment has no related notes, the page shows a create a new note text link where the list of linked notes would be. Works in light and dark mode.

## Why

The user asked on 2026-11-18 to see today's appointments at a glance and reach their notes in one click.

## Notes

Requested 2026-11-18. Related notes are found the way the Today dashboard finds them today (CurrentPanel).
