# 169 Todos linked to people

## What

Desktop app, plus a CLI command where it fits. `@[Full Name]` or one of the person's aliases in a todo text (same rules as in notes) links the todo to that person. The person page lists the open todos that mention them, then the done ones, and the todo list shows the mention as a link to the person page. Old todos load as before.

## Why

Many todos are about someone (ask Anna, send Ben the draft); with them on the person page I have everything for that person in one place before we talk.
