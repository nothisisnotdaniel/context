# 061 Resurfacing

As a Context user, I want old notes brought back now and then, so that useful knowledge doesn't get buried and stale notes get cleaned up.

## What

The desktop app shows "on this day" notes (same date in earlier years and months, from daily notes and note dates) and once a week a short pick of notes not opened for months, each with Keep, Archive (moves it to `archive/`) and Dismiss. Builds on recent notes for the last-opened dates.

## Why

Notes I don't open for months are effectively lost. "On this day" and a weekly pick with Keep, Archive and Dismiss keep the collection alive and tidy.
