# 427 Hint Cmd or Ctrl click in the web link title

As a Context user, I want the link hover title to tell me how to open the link, so that I find out without reading the README.

Category: ux

## What

Add a short Cmd+click (macOS) or Ctrl+click hint to the hover title of web links in the editor, next to the URL; this changes AC-7 of job 354, which keeps the title equal to the href.

## Why

Raised by review-ui and review-ux in job 354: the title shows only the URL, so Cmd/Ctrl+click is discoverable only through the README.
