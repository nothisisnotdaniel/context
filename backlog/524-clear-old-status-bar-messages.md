# 524 Clear old status bar messages

Category: ux

## What

Clear an old message from the status bar after a while, or when the user moves to another page, so a stale "Saving…" or an old result does not stay visible. Errors stay until the next action. Screen reader announcements stay as they are.

## Why

The status bar from #506 keeps every message until the next one, so old feedback can look current. From the 455 UX review.
