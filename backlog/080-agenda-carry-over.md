# 080 Agenda carry-over

As a Context user, I want open points from the last meeting copied into the next meeting's note, so that nothing from the previous meeting gets dropped.

## What

A new meeting note for an occurrence of a recurring meeting copies the still-open points (`- [ ]` lines and a section named "Open points") from the note of the previous occurrence under "From last time", with a link to that note. The old note is never changed. Builds on meeting notes and recurring appointments.

## Why

Recurring meetings often leave points open, and re-reading the previous note to find them is easy to skip. The old note is never changed.
