# 081 Topics for the next 1:1

As a Context user, I want to note topics for my next meeting with someone and see them when that meeting comes up, so that I raise everything I meant to.

## What

A line `next: @[Full Name] <topic>` anywhere is a topic to raise with that person. Open topics show on the person page and, when a meeting with that person as attendee is next (or a meeting note with them is created), at the top of that meeting's view and note. A topic is ticked off like a todo. Stored in the index (a parser version bump). Builds on people pages and meeting notes.

## Why

Things to discuss with someone come up days before the meeting and are forgotten by then.
