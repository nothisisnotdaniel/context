# 488 AI writing help

As someone writing a note or a mail draft, I want AI to rewrite a selected passage, so that I get a clearer version without leaving the editor.

Category: feature

## What

In the editor, a selection offers `Rewrite` (clearer, shorter, more formal) through the AI layer; the result shows next to the original and replaces it only on Accept, as one undo step.

## Why

assistant has writing help. It fits the editor once the AI layer exists.
