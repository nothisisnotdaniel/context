# 487 AI task suggestions with evidence

As someone with long meeting notes, I want AI to suggest todos and show the line each comes from, so that I can add them with one click and trust where they came from.

Category: feature

## What

On a note, `Suggest todos` sends the text through the AI layer and lists suggested todos, each with the quoted line it is based on (checked to be in the note; a suggestion without a matching line is dropped). Each one can be added as a stored todo or a note todo, or ignored.

## Why

assistant suggests tasks with evidence. The evidence line keeps the suggestions honest.
