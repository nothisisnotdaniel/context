# 486 AI meeting summary

As someone after a meeting, I want an AI summary of my meeting note, so that I can share the outcome without writing it again.

Category: feature

## What

On a meeting note, `Summarize` sends the note's text (through the shared AI layer's preview and budget) and shows the summary; the user can insert it into the note or copy it. Nothing is written without the user's click.

## Why

assistant offers meeting summaries. With the AI layer in place it is a small feature.
