# 087 Standup notes

As a Context and Times user, I want my standup (yesterday, today, blocked) built with one button, so that I don't piece it together every morning.

## What

One button (and a CLI command) builds "Yesterday" (Times checkpoints of the last workday, read-only, and the done log), "Today" (todos due and today's meetings) and "Blocked" (open waiting-for items), as plain text to copy into Teams or into the daily note. Builds on the done log, the waiting-for list and Times bookings in /current.

## Why

All three parts already exist in Times bookings, the done log, todos, meetings and the waiting-for list.
