# 084 Screenshot to inbox

As a Context user, I want a hotkey that captures a screen area into my inbox, so that screenshots get triaged like any other capture.

## What

A global hotkey in the desktop app lets the user pick a screen area; the image is saved to `attachments/` and a time-stamped inbox line links it. Also a watched folder setting, so screenshots saved by ShareX or CleanShot X land in the inbox the same way. Builds on quick capture and attachments.

## Why

Screenshots pile up on the desktop with no context. Saving them as attachments with an inbox line, also from ShareX or CleanShot X, puts them into the normal flow.
