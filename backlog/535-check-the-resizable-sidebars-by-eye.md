# 535 Check the resizable sidebars by eye on macOS

Category: ui

## What

Drag both sidebar handles, collapse and expand each side with mouse and keyboard, restart the app and check that widths and collapsed state come back; check the focus ring on the resize handle in light and dark theme.

## Why

Job 490 tested resizing and saving in code, but drag feel, the collapsed rail and the handle focus state can only be judged on screen.
