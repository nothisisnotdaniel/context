# 093 Phone companion app

As the user, I want to decide later on a small phone app for capture, voice notes, Times check-in and today's view, so that the most common actions work away from the computer.

## What

A small new app (its own project via `/factory init`) for iPhone and Android with only quick capture, voice notes, Times check in and out, and a read-only view of today. Syncs through the same folders, no server of its own; .NET MAUI to reuse `Context.Core`, or a light web app. Decide after the desktop move.

## Why

It would be a new project with its own platform choices (MAUI or a web app), so it waits for the desktop move.
