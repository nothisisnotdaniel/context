# 491 Earlier meetings of a series on the appointment page

As someone going into a recurring meeting, I want to see the earlier occurrences and their notes, so that I can pick up where we left off.

Category: feature

## What

The appointment page of a recurring meeting lists earlier occurrences of the same series (same Uid) that have a meeting note, newest first, with the date and a link to each note, at most 20 then `… <n> more`.

## Why

assistant shows a series history. Context already has meeting notes per occurrence and the carry-over, but no list.
