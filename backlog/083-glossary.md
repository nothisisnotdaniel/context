# 083 Glossary

As a Context user, I want terms defined as `Term:: meaning` collected into a glossary, so that I can look up jargon and see its meaning on hover.

## What

Lines `Term:: meaning` in any note are collected (in the index, a parser version bump) into a Glossary view, sorted and searchable, with the note each came from. In the desktop app, a known term in a note shows its meaning on hover. Same term in several notes: all meanings are listed.

## Why

Work is full of acronyms and project names, and their definitions end up scattered.
