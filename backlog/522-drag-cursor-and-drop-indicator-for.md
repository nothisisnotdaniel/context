# 522 Drag cursor and drop indicator for pinned notes

As a user, I want pinned rows to show a grab cursor and where a dragged note will land, so that dragging feels clear.

Category: ui

## What

Show cursor: grab on pinned rows and a drop highlight (line or row tint) on the target while dragging; clear it on drop, dragend and leave.

## Why

Drag and drop on pinned notes works today but gives no visual cue.
