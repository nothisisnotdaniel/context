# 523 Keep focus on a pinned row after Alt+Arrow

As a keyboard user, I want focus to stay on the pinned note I just moved, so that I can press Alt+Arrow again without re-focusing.

Category: ux

## What

After an Alt+ArrowUp/Down move, put focus back on the moved row name button once the list re-renders.

## Why

Focus is lost after each move, so repeated moves need a click or Tab.
