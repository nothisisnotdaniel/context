# 374 Search results on their own full-size page

As the user, I want search results to open on their own full-size page, so that I can read and scan many results instead of a small popover list.

## What

Search from the top bar (job 218, #271) shows results in a popover. Show them on a full-size search page in the main area instead: the query field at the top, results with title, page type, matching snippet with the hit highlighted and the date, keyboard navigation, and opening a result opens the page. Back returns to the results with the query kept. Saved searches (#68) open the same page.

## Why

The user asked for search results on their own full-size page: the popover is too small to scan results.
