# 477 Calendar markers for notes and appointments

As someone picking a day in the calendar, I want to see which days have notes and which have appointments, so that I can find the day I am looking for at a glance.

Category: ui

## What

The sidebar calendar (and the journal view's date picker) marks each day with a daily or meeting note and each day with an appointment, with two different markers that also differ by shape, not only colour, and have an accessible label (for example `has notes, 3 appointments`).

## Why

Today every day in the calendar looks the same, so finding the day of a meeting means clicking around. assistant marks notes and appointments differently.
